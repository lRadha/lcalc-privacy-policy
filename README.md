
# lCalc – Privacy Policy

Official privacy policy for **lCalc**, an offline investment profit and loss calculator for Android.

## About lCalc

lCalc helps users calculate investment profits and losses using manually entered investment amounts, purchase prices, and selling prices.

The application is designed with privacy in mind.

## Privacy Overview

- No user registration or login.
- No personal information collection.
- No internet communication or remote servers.
- No advertisements.
- No analytics or tracking services.
- No third-party data sharing.
- No sensitive Android permissions.
- All calculations are performed locally on the device.
- Calculation inputs are temporary and are not saved as history.
