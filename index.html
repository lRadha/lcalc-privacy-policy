1. Introduction

lCalc is an investment profit and loss calculator developed and published by an individual developer. The application helps users calculate investment returns, profits, and losses using manually entered investment amounts, purchase prices, and selling prices.

Your privacy is important to us. lCalc is designed to operate locally on your device without collecting or transmitting personal information.

2. Information Collection

lCalc does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

The application does not require:

  • Registration or account creation.
  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Location information.
  • Contacts, photographs, or media.
  • Banking or brokerage account access.

3. Calculation Data

Users may enter financial values such as investment amounts, purchase prices, selling prices, and other values required for calculations.

All entered values are processed locally on your device. These values are held temporarily while using the application.

They are not uploaded to a server, transmitted to third parties, or saved as persistent calculation history.

4. Permissions

lCalc does not request Android device permissions to perform its calculation functions.

The application does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other personal device resources.

5. Advertising and Analytics

lCalc does not contain advertisements.

The application does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party tracking services.

6. Data Sharing

No personal information or calculation data is shared with third parties.

The application does not operate a remote server or cloud-based data collection system.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

lCalc does not maintain user accounts or store calculation history.

Calculation inputs are used temporarily during an active application session and are discarded when no longer needed. No remote user data is retained because the application does not transmit user data to external servers.

8. Data Security

Since calculation data is processed locally and is not transmitted to external servers, lCalc does not maintain a remote database containing user information.

Users should protect access to their own devices using appropriate device security features.

9. Children's Privacy

lCalc does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or privacy practices change.

Any revisions will be published at the same privacy policy URL with an updated revision date.
